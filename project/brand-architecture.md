# Brand architecture

**mod** is the master brand. Each business area (affärsområde, AO) that meets the market has its own main colour and its own logo lockup; complement and accent colours are shared by all of them.

| Brand | Main scale | Stands for | Tokens |
|---|---|---|---|
| mod | Blue (BLÅ) | responsibility, security, trust | `blue-1` … `blue-5` |
| Insikt | Purple (LILA) | success, creativity, intelligence | `purple-1` … `purple-5` |
| Construct | Green (GRÖN) | calm, strength, growth | `green-1` … `green-5` |
| Concept | Red (RÖD) | power, passion, creativity | `red-1` … `red-5` |
| Service | uses mod blue | internal support to every area, not market-facing | `blue-*` |
| Kapitel | not in the manual | ask before using any colour for it | — |

Each scale has five steps. Step 1 is the strongest and is the one the logo uses; text and other elements may take any step of the brand's own scale. Use a light step on a dark ground and a dark step on a light ground.

## Which brand leads: the logo hierarchies

1. **mod leads** ("ETT mod"): the mod blocks above the area's name. The default.
2. **The area leads**, mod second.
3. **Separated**: mod and the area apart on the same surface.
4. **Graphic element and the area's name only.**

Default to hierarchy 1. Each brand uses its own scale for its logo and typography; never render a logo in another area's colour.

## Taglines

*Built to inspire* is mod's. The areas complete the same phrase: *Developed to inspire* (Concept), *Constructed to inspire* (Construct), *Digitalized to inspire* (Insikt), plus *Tailored to inspire* and *Optimized to inspire*. Short headlines may be set in capitals for impact; longer ones in sentence case.

## The 50 / 30 / 20 story

mod's method promises 50% less time and cost in idea, design and planning, 30% shorter production time and 20% lower lifetime cost of a building. The same split may divide a page or a slide.

## Internal web apps

The web apps in this system are internal tools, so they carry the mod master brand (the blue hero) whatever area uses them. An area's colour appears inside the content when the content is that area's own (an Insikt annual report is purple); company lists always run Concept, Construct, Service, Insikt.
