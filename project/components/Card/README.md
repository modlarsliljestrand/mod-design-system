# Card

The white panel for page content: 18px corners, a hairline border, the soft `shadow`, 22px / 24px padding and a 16.5px Bold title.

- One card holds one thing: a title (`h3`) and what belongs to it. Body text is 14px; secondary text takes `.muted`.
- Stacked cards get 16px between them (`.card + .card`).
- `.cols` puts two cards side by side, a wide left column (1.6fr) and a right column of at least 300px; one column under 900px.
- On phones the padding tightens to 18px / 16px.
