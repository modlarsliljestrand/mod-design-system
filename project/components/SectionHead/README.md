# SectionHead

A section title with its context: the `h2` (22px Bold), a muted tag after it, and a hairline rule that fills the rest of the row.

- Use it above every group of tiles or cards: `section.section` > `.section__head` > `h2` + `span.tag` + `span.rule`, then the `.grid`.
- The tag says who or what the section is for, in lower case: "byggda med Claude", "kräver behörighet", "det vi loggar in i varje dag".
- Sections sit 40px apart (`section-gap`), 26px on phones.
