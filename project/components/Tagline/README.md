# Tagline

The BUILT TO INSPIRE pill: mod's master tagline in `blue-1` capitals on `accent` lime, after a small square bullet.

- Once per portal home page, as the last line of the hero. Write it in sentence case in the markup (`Built to inspire`); the class sets the capitals and the 0.14em tracking.
- The master line is mod's. A business area may use its own line from the manual: *Developed to inspire* (Concept), *Constructed to inspire* (Construct), *Digitalized to inspire* (Insikt), or *Tailored* / *Optimized to inspire*.
- It is a label: no link, no hover, no icon.
- The pill reads the same on the hero and on `bg` because both of its colours are fixed brand tokens.
